{# F102: the AI classification under way — a narrow pill at the bottom of the page. It is in every page of the app (base.html) and the classification goes on from page to page: its state is kept in the browser tab (sessionStorage), and each page that opens picks it up where it stopped. Two kinds of job: - "register": the transactions of the register; at the end the review page opens (from any page); - "import": the rows of an import preview; the preview applies the suggestions as they come, and when it is left the pill offers «Torna all'import» (the preview is kept for a while, see export.bank_reopen). `ai_progress_window()` and `ai_progress_script()` once, in base.html. #} {% macro ai_progress_window() %}
